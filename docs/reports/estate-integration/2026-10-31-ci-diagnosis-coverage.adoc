== 1. What squabbler says it is (the canon that binds this investigation)

From this repository's own `ANCHOR`/`ECOSYSTEM` statements and
`docs/CHARTER.adoc`:

*IS*:: a standalone per-repo CI/CD **fighter**. It drives a stuck branch
protection gate to a *legitimately satisfied* green by satisfying its required
checks, or improves red→yellow, or fails and emits a structured report.

*IS-NOT*:: a bypass (never `enforce_admins=off`, never drop a required context,
never admin-merge) · hypatia-dependent · a red→green code-fixer · a merge-commit
lander.

Two further docs constrain its *rank*, not just its scope, and they are the
reason this report is mostly about **handoffs** rather than features:

. `.machine_readable/bot_directives/gate_triage.a2ml` declares a precedence
  order: `gitbot-fleet/robot-repo-automaton` is **primary** wherever it is
  "present AND working", and `cicd-squabbler` is the **fallback**, bound by
  `fallback-must-be-standalone = true`. The squabbler exists for repos that do
  not adopt the full stack, or for the estate when the primary is down, polluted
  or out of paid CI minutes.
. The directive also fixes the invariant that shapes every answer below: an
  unavailable gate **classifies as `escalate`, never `self-win`**, and
  `may-transition-to-green = false`.

So the honest role statement is: **squabbler is the per-repo *gate-semantics*
specialist and the estate's fallback classifier. It explains why a required
context is absent or unsatisfiable, and who must act. It does not own runner
provisioning, Actions settings, estate inventory, policy findings, or evidence
provenance — and it must not pretend to.**

== 2. The five outcomes, and who decides each

The estate investigation separates these outcomes because collapsing them is how
a red board becomes a lie:

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
| # | Outcome

| 1 | *Not started* — workflow admission or source resolution failed; zero jobs, no
log, no check run.
| 2 | *Preflight* — a job started and died in runner setup, provisioning or lock
resolution, **before any policy step executed**.
| 3 | *Prerequisite* — a tool, credential or configuration fact was missing, so the
policy step could not run.
| 4 | *Executed finding* — the policy/security/test actually ran and reported
something.
| 5 | *Verified success* — the policy ran and passed, with evidence.
|===

Nobody in the estate owns all five. The division of labour, measured from the
sibling repositories' own canon:

[cols="1,2,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Outcome | Diagnoses | Remediation of record | Never

| 1 | `squabble zero-job` (per-run attribution), `squabble chains` (moved
upstreams *before* a run proves it) · hypatia `scripts/ci-health` `B-ALLOWLIST`,
`B-STARTUPFAIL`, `B-LOCKFILE` · standards `allowlist-preflight-reusable.yml`,
`lockfile-drift-detect.yml`
| hypatia `remediate.sh` PUTs the curated superset (`action-superset.txt`), then
reads the setting back; settings drift by standards
`settings-drift-detect.yml`; a human clears billing
| A workflow edit is never the fix for an allow-list refusal; no bot infers a
cause from the conclusion alone

| 2 | *nobody, from step evidence* — hypatia reads **job annotations**
(`B-LOCKFILE`), and `A-BILLING` is an annotation class too
| owner of the provisioning (toolchain, lock regeneration with the authoritative
tool, runner label, billing)
| A provisioning failure is never reported as a code/security finding

| 3 | `cicd-squabbler` for the *policy-shaped* subset (Actions posture, SHA
pinning, allow-list); otherwise the owner's own preflight
| owner of settings/secrets; standards `provisioning-check-reusable.yml`
| A missing secret is never reported as a scan finding, and a missing tool is
never reported as a pass

| 4 | the producing policy (cicd-suite gates, hypatia SARIF, tests) ·
`cicd-squabbler` only *routes* it (`EscalateToExpert`)
| hypatia + fleet (`hypatia-mcp`/`fleet-mcp`) where adopted; the repo's own owner
otherwise
| squabbler never fixes code to turn a finding green (`IS-NOT`)

| 5 | `Gate::evaluate` + `polarity` (vacuity) + `contracts/squabble`
`legitimate_green` · proven-tests-and-benches provenance tiers for *test* claims
| nobody: it is either evidenced or it is not
| A skipped, unavailable, vacuous or unmeasured check is never green
|===

== 3. Coverage matrix: what squabbler can detect and explain today

Verdicts: *covered* (detects **and** explains), *partial* (detects, explains
weakly, or covers one sub-case), *gap* (neither).

=== 3.1 Outcome 1 — not started (zero jobs)

*Covered, and better than any sibling on the two attribution classes.*

* `squabble_core::startup` models `StartupFailureClass::{MovedCoordinate,
  AllowListAdmissionRequired, Unattributed}` with **distinct moves**
  (`RepointToCanonical` carrying the same pin; `AllowListAdmissionRequired`
  naming the minimal `owner/repo@*`). `AllowList::covers` /
  `missing_pattern` / `admission_unknown` encode the `selected` semantics,
  including `github_owned_allowed` and the `verified_allowed` blind spot, so an
  unresolvable creator status degrades to *unknown*, never to *refused*.
* `squabble zero-job` reports per run: repo, workflow, coordinate as written,
  canonical target, required pattern, and the sequencing (repoint first, then
  admit). Exit `4` on *any* observed zero-job run — including unattributed ones,
  "because silence is not a clean result".
* `squabble chains` resolves every upstream through REST with redirects followed
  and compares canonical `full_name`s — **names, never pins** — so a
  renamed/transferred upstream is named before a run proves it.
* `MissingCause` in `squabble_core::gate` keeps the causes of a missing context
  distinct — `NoSuchJob`, `DeadActionPin`, `ActionsPolicyRefusal`,
  `MovedUpstreamCoordinate`, `AllowListAdmissionRequired`, `EmptyJobsMap`,
  `NotTriggeredOnPullRequest`, and an explicit `Unknown` — each with its own
  remedy string, and a test asserting that *no* cause can move a gate to green.

*What it does not explain:* the two causes whose evidence is not a workflow file
or an allow-list — **billing refusal (`A-BILLING`)** and **lockfile drift
(`B-LOCKFILE`)**. These land in `Unattributed` with the probe failure quoted.
That is the correct fail-closed answer, and it is also where this repository's
own CI sits (`.github/settings.yml`: "GitHub Actions is billing-blocked for
PRIVATE repositories on this account … which is why every workflow here fails
with zero steps"). The report says "unattributed"; it does not say *which sibling
already knows the answer*. That is gap *G-4*.

=== 3.2 Outcome 2 — preflight (runner setup / provisioning / lock)

*Gap — the largest one. It was not detectable from the gate model, and until
this change there was no notion of it anywhere in the engine.*

Why: the engine's only input per requirement is `RequiredCheck { run: CheckRun }`
(`CheckRun::{Missing, Pending, Failed, Skipped, Passed}`). A job that dies in
`Set up job`, or in `Install Rust toolchain`, arrives as `CheckRun::Failed` —
*the same value as a genuine failing test*. `squabble_fight::workflows::
classify_with_policy` then attributes the red from the **shape of the workflow**:

* reusable-in-another-repo → `AssignGateOwner{OwnedUpstream}`;
* `CodeBuild` → `EscalateToExpert{HypatiaFleet, DispatchFix}`;
* `Security` → `EscalateToExpert{Security, Scan}`;
* `Hygiene` → `GroundTruthCheckNames`.

For a preflight failure every one of those is a *misattribution*: nothing has
been said about the code, the scan, or the reusable. The estate has measured
both shapes:

* `A-BILLING` — "the job was not started because recent account payments have
  failed…", which manifests on code-scanning gates as "waiting for results from
  CodeQL" because no result is ever uploaded (hypatia `scripts/ci-health`,
  class `A-BILLING`).
* `B-LOCKFILE` — an `actions.lock` verification failure, diagnosed by reading
  **job annotations** (same source, class `B-LOCKFILE`; standards
  `lockfile-drift-detect.yml` reports estate-wide and opens one tracking issue,
  never mutating another repository).

Note the two sources disagree about the *mechanism* and this matters: standards'
`lockfile-drift-detect.yml` header says a drifted lock produces `startup_failure`
with **zero jobs**, while hypatia's ci-health classifies lock failures from
**job annotations** in failed runs. Both can be true (the failure can be
recorded either way depending on where it is refused), which is precisely why
neither conclusion alone is a diagnosis — and hypatia states the rule out loud:
"Do not infer an allow-list defect from the conclusion alone."

*Implemented in this change:* `squabble_core::execution` (see §10) classifies
step evidence into a distinct `PreflightFailed` class, and
`squabble phase <job.json>` prints it with an owner handoff. It is **not yet
wired into `fight`** — that needs a jobs fetch for failed checks (*P-2*).

=== 3.3 Outcome 3 — missing tools, credentials or configuration

*Partial.* Split three ways today:

* *Actions posture* — well covered and first-class:
  `ReconcileActionsPolicy`, `SetActionsAllowedAll`, `PinWorkflowActions`,
  `AllowListAdmissionRequired`, plus a **latent** scan (`structural_scan` in
  `squabble_fight::lib`) that surfaces an allow-list gap or a tag-pinned `uses:`
  *before* a run proves it.
* *Tools* — `polarity` deliberately refuses to model them: `Recommendation`
  omits the directive's case 4 (`missing-local-tooling`) because it "is not
  observable from the jobs API". Also `VacuitySignature::is_usable` requires both
  halves, so a skipped scan step with no stub-writing step is read as a
  legitimately optional step rather than vacuity.
* *Credentials/configuration* — **not modelled at all** before this change, and
  still only partly: `squabble_core::execution::PrerequisiteMissing` fires only
  for a **host-declared** prerequisite step. The shipped directive declares one,
  because only one was measured (see `.machine_readable/bot_directives/
  execution_taxonomy.a2ml`). Everything else stays in the policy lane, which is
  the honest default.

The estate's own example of the shape: standards' `cicd-squabbler-reusable.yml`
probes whether a supplied credential can read the whole gate before asking
`squabble fight` to classify anything, and reports **NOT RUN** (no credential) or
**NOT EVALUATED** (credential present, gate only partly visible) rather than a
verdict.

=== 3.4 Outcome 4 — genuine executed findings

*Covered as a handoff, by construction.* `Move::EscalateToExpert` and
`Move::AssignGateOwner` are non-satisfying: `Report` buckets each unsatisfied
check as self-win / escalation / owner assignment, and neither of the latter two
constructs a `CheckRun` or drops a context. `--summon` (feature `boj`) puts
expert verdicts on the report and **fails closed** when an expert is
unreachable.

*What it does not do — deliberately:* decide whether a finding is real. The
report routes. `Move::FlagNonFunctionalGate` recognises the unwinnable case
(mined from `hypatia#566`: a required check whose own script fails
unconditionally) so the squabbler stops re-diagnosing it every run.

=== 3.5 Outcome 5 — verified success

*Covered, with the distinction the estate keeps asking for.* Three layers, and
they are not interchangeable:

. `Gate::evaluate` → `Green` iff the requirement set is **non-empty** and every
  requirement `is_satisfied()`. `is_satisfied()` accepts `Passed` **or**
  `Skipped`, mirroring GitHub's ruleset. An empty requirement set is `Blocked` —
  the one-line proof that dropping contexts is not a path to green.
. `Gate::evidence_free()` lists the `Skipped` requirements that satisfied the
  gate while proving nothing, and `Contract` §5 makes the same distinction
  upstream: `legitimate_green` requires *every* required check to have run and
  passed, and there are negative fixtures for `skipped`
  (`gate/required-skipped.bound.json`, `gate/passed-and-skipped.bound.json`).
  So a `Green` gate is explicitly **not** a `legitimate_green`.
. `squabble_core::polarity` interrogates a *green* check for vacuity
  (`StubbedAfterSkippedScan`, `AllStepsSkipped`) and routes a finding to the
  owner-facing `GateTriage` group — the only escalation group that names no
  estate service, because the obligation is the owner's.

The new `squabble_core::execution::Verified` sits at layer 2.5: a job passed
**and** a policy step recorded success. It is deliberately *not* a gate verdict
(`handoff() == NoClaim`) and must be read with `polarity`.

=== 3.6 Summary

[cols="3,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Outcome | Verdict | Where

| 1 not started | covered (2 of 4 causes) | `squabble-core::startup`, `squabble zero-job`, `squabble chains`
| 2 preflight | **gap → new classifier, unwired** | `squabble-core::execution` (§10)
| 3 prerequisites | partial | Actions-policy moves; `PrerequisiteMissing` with a deliberately thin signature
| 4 executed findings | covered as routing | `Move::EscalateToExpert`, `Report`
| 5 verified success | covered, layered | `Gate::evaluate`, `evidence_free`, `polarity`, contract §5
|===

== 4. Does it preserve required contexts?

Yes, and this is the invariant everything else defers to:

* No `Move` variant removes or renames a requirement. `ReconcileRequiredContext`
  changes the *required* name to match a real emitted name — a ruleset edit, not
  a workflow weakening — and is propose-only.
* `Gate::evaluate` cannot reach `Green` from an empty requirement set
  (`empty_requirement_set_is_never_green`), and `a_diagnosed_missing_check_still
  _cannot_reach_green` iterates every `MissingCause` asserting none reaches
  `Green`.
* The new classifier has no path to `Green`: it builds no `CheckRun`, calls no
  `evaluate`, and proposes no `Move`.
* `zero-job`, `chains` and `phase` are all read-only, and stand outside
  `squabble-contract/1.0.0` (whose surface stays at four subcommands). Nothing
  here adds meaning to the pinned revision.

One honest asymmetry to keep visible: **the gate model and the contract disagree
by design.** GitHub satisfies a requirement with `SKIPPED`; `legitimate_green`
does not. A consumer that reads only `squabble fetch`'s `Green` can therefore
believe more than the contract certifies. The mitigation is `evidence_free()`
being populated and printed — not a redefinition of either verdict.

== 5. Source and publication dependencies

`chains` models **source** dependencies: `EdgeKind::{ReusableWorkflow, Action}`,
pins, canonical names, cycles and dead upstreams. It does not model
**publication** dependencies, and those are what actually broke this repository
on 2026-10-28 (`docs/reports/maintenance/2026-10-28-zero-job-allowlist.adoc`):

[cols="2,3,1",options="header"]
|===
| Dependency | Evidence it is load-bearing | In `chains`?

| owner/repo *move* of a reusable (`hyperpolymath/standards` →
`metadatastician/standards`) | 7 workflows died as zero-job `startup_failure`; the
discriminator was the *callee's owner*, nothing else in the caller changed |
partly — canonical resolution catches the move, but the *allow-list precedence*
it triggers is not an edge

| the curated allow-list superset, published as `action-superset.txt` in hypatia
`scripts/ci-health/` | its README: the superset was updated for the 2026-10-25
move, "this repository's own allow-list evidently was not" | **no** — a
cross-repo published artifact every repo's settings derive from

| the squabble release binary + build attestation, consumed by
`metadatastician/standards` as `.github/actions/squabble@1ab1507a…` | that
reusable refuses any binary whose sha256/attestation do not match its pinned
release | **no** — a release is not a `uses:` edge from a *consumer repo* that
`chains` scans

| `contracts/squabble` and `contracts/proven-tests` (revision + `SHASUMS`) |
`contracts/bin/verify-pin.sh` exists precisely because consumers pin by digest |
**no**

| the reusable's *content* at a pin | a SHA pin is immutable; a tag pin is not —
`chains` warns on tag/branch pins for this reason | yes
|===

*Gap G-5:* a consumer's install of the squabble binary can fail (attestation
mismatch, missing release asset) as a **class-2 preflight failure inside the
consumer's job**, and no squabbler surface describes that dependency. The
composite action's own self-test (`tests/squabble_action_test.sh`, 27 cases) is
the only place it is checked.

== 6. Owner handoffs for inaccessible settings

The measured situation: the built-in `GITHUB_TOKEN` cannot read the settings that
decide outcome 1 and 3. This repository's own 2026-10-28 report records
`403 Resource not accessible by integration` on every `actions/permissions*`
endpoint, and standards' reusable records the same for classic branch protection
("the classic-protection endpoint is 403 to the built-in Actions token even
though all 24 ruleset contexts are visible"). The behaviour that follows is
correct and worth stating plainly: **`fight` refuses to reason about a partially
visible gate** (exit 2 with "classic branch protection on this branch is not
visible to this token", `squabble-cli/src/fetch.rs`), and the reusable converts
that into NOT RUN / NOT EVALUATED rather than an all-clear.

What is *not* precise today is the handoff itself. The squabbler names the
minimal pattern and says "no workflow edit can make the run start", but does not
name **the principal and the surface**:

[cols="2,2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Setting | Read/write surface | Principal who must act | What squabbler says today

| Actions allow-list (`allowed_actions`, `patterns_allowed`) |
`GET/PUT repos/{o}/{r}/actions/permissions[/selected-actions]` — `administration:
read` / `administration: write` | *repo admin* for the repo; **org admin** when an
org policy sets the ceiling | the exact `owner/repo@*` pattern + "settings are the
owner's realm" (precise on the *what*, silent on the *who*)

| SHA-pinning requirement | same endpoints | repo admin (an org policy can
re-impose it) | "keep `sha_pinning_required=true`" + `PinWorkflowActions`

| Branch ruleset / required contexts | `GET/PUT repos/{o}/{r}/rulesets/...`,
`.../rules/branches/{b}` — `administration: read` (write to change) | repo admin |
`ReconcileRequiredContext` as a propose-only move

| Classic branch protection | `.../branches/{b}/protection` — 403 to
`GITHUB_TOKEN`, 404 when absent | repo admin | distinguishes 403 from 404 in the
error and refuses to classify on 403

| Billing / spending limit | *no API this tool holds* | **account owner** only |
nothing — it degrades to `Unattributed` (correct, but unhelpful: see *G-4*)

| Secrets existence | `GET .../actions/secrets` (names only) | repo admin |
not modelled at all (*G-2*)

| Runner label / group availability | no *readable* surface for the queue reason
| repo or org admin | not modelled (*G-2*)
|===

*Gap G-3:* the report names a fix but not a principal. The estate currently
compensates in workflow glue (standards' probe + NOT EVALUATED). That is the
right place for the *permission* decision, but the *report* should carry the
principal, so a human reading a fight report knows whether to page an org admin,
a repo admin or the billing owner. `squabble_core::execution::Handoff` now
carries an owner phrase per class as a first step (§10); `fight`'s report does
not yet.

== 7. Coordination: no competing rewrites

The estate already has overlapping observers. The rules below are *derived from
what each repository declares*, not invented here.

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Sibling | What it owns | The rule for squabbler

| `gitbot-fleet` / `robot-repo-automaton` | present-and-working primary for all
five cases, with its own override rules under `.machine_readable/bot_directives/
rr_automaton/` | yields. Squabbler is the fallback, and must keep working with
the fleet absent (`fallback-must-be-standalone`)

| `hypatia` (`scripts/ci-health/`) | class detection + **auto-remediation** of
`B-ALLOWLIST` (PUT the curated superset, read back, verify), `D-BURN` (opens
scoped PRs), `A-BILLING`/`B-LOCKFILE`/`B-STARTUPFAIL` (report only);
`baton-bridge.sh` runs checks on owned compute when GitHub CI is blocked | never
PUTs a setting; never opens the D-BURN PR; **names the minimal pattern for one
run** and must not propose a divergent superset. The superset of record is
`hypatia/scripts/ci-health/action-superset.txt`

| `standards` (reusable gates) | the *wiring*: `allowlist-preflight-reusable.yml`
(tree + credentialed live policy), `actions-lock-gate.yml`,
`lock-sync-gate.yml`, `lockfile-drift-detect.yml`, `required-contexts.yml` (+
`.machine_readable/required-contexts-allow.txt` as a **declaration**, not a
silencer), `settings-drift-detect.yml`, `check-suite-monitor.yml`, and the
`cicd-squabbler-reusable.yml` consumer contract | squabbler supplies diagnosis to
these; it does not re-implement a preflight, does not edit workflows from CI
(`--apply` is refused by the consumer contract), and does not add an allow-list
"silencer" of its own

| `cicd-suite` (30 composite audit actions) | the *policy* checks (required-files,
SPDX, hygiene, manifests, language gates) with an explicit tiering: enforcing /
advisory / **tool-dependent (`not_run`, exit 2 — "NO CHECK WAS PERFORMED")** /
unimplemented | consumes their `not_run` distinction as evidence for outcome 3;
never re-runs their logic; never reports their `not_run` as a finding *or* a pass

| `reposystem` | estate inventory / coordination layer (`repos.toml`
regenerated from `.gitmodules`), megasweep control plane | squabbler never
invents repo identity; the API owner is the identity (`drive off the API owner,
never local dir names` is already a hypatia lesson), and `chains` takes
`path=owner/repo`

| `proven-tests-and-benches` | evidence provenance for *test* claims
(`Actually-Proven` / `Provisionally-Proven` / `Unproven`; coverage derived only
from tests that ran and passed) | squabbler does not assert test evidence; its
`contracts/proven-tests` contract is the submission format, and a `Verified`
phase class is **not** a provenance tier
|===

Two failure modes this report explicitly forbids:

* **Two bots rewriting one workflow.** Only a human (or a separately reviewed
  automation with its own reviewed mandate) edits workflows. Squabbler's
  `--apply` enacts one local edit (strip a `on.*.paths` filter that strands a
  required check), never commits, never pushes, and is **refused** by the
  consumer contract in CI.
* **Two bots "fixing" the same setting with different values.** The allow-list
  has one source of record (hypatia's superset). A narrow addition named by
  squabbler's report is a *request*, not a competing write.

== 9. What was *not* changed

* No required context was dropped, renamed away, or reconciled.
* No policy, allow-list, ruleset or pinning posture was disabled or loosened.
* No issue or PR was closed, and nothing was re-run to make a board look better.
* No new exit code means "green"; `squabble phase` has only `0` and `2`.
* `squabble-contract/1.0.0` is untouched: its four subcommands and the
  `legitimate_green` decision procedure are unchanged, and `phase` — like
  `zero-job` and `chains` — is outside the contract surface.

== 10. Implemented in this change

=== 10.1 `squabble_core::execution` — the execution-phase classifier

New pure module, no new dependency, no `Move` variant, no gate change.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Class | Meaning

| `NotStarted` | no job exists (outcome 1) — attribution deferred to `startup`
| `PreflightFailed{step}` | a declared setup/provisioning step (or GitHub's
implicit `Set up job`) concluded failure: **provisioning, not a finding**
| `PrerequisiteMissing{step}` | a declared tool/credential/config step failed, so
the policy step could not run
| `PolicyStepFailed{step}` | a policy step ran and failed — *where*, not *why*
| `Verified{step}` | the job passed **and** a policy step recorded success
| `Unclassified{reason}` | fail-closed non-answer, with one of five reasons
|===

Design notes, all of which are tested:

* **Signatures are host-supplied** (`PhaseSignatures`), never compiled in — the
  same standalone discipline as `polarity::VacuitySignature`. Matching is
  **exact** after trimming, because this repository has already paid once for
  abbreviation drift (`gate_triage.a2ml` 0.2.0 → 0.3.0).
* **Evidence tier stated**: step names and conclusions only. The module docs say
  outright that a dependency-resolution error inside `Cargo check --locked` is
  indistinguishable from a compile failure here, so `PolicyStepFailed` must not
  be read as "class 4 confirmed". Job annotations are the stronger source, and
  they belong to the sibling that owns them.
* **Ordering**: preflight is checked before prerequisite, and both before
  policy, so nothing later can mask an earlier stop. A failure recorded only by
  `Post job`/`Complete job` is *not* a policy finding.
* **No green**: no `CheckRun` is constructed, `Verified` hands off to `NoClaim`,
  and the tests assert every class serialises without a gate state.

=== 10.2 Host wiring

* `.machine_readable/bot_directives/execution_taxonomy.a2ml` — the step
  signatures, each with the workflow it was read from on 2026-10-31, and an
  explicit note that `cargo check --locked` is **not** a setup step.
* `squabble_fight::execution::{load_signatures, parse_signatures}` — fail-safe
  reader scoped to `[execution-taxonomy]` so no other section can donate keys;
  an absent directive yields an unusable set ("claim no preflight class").
* `squabble phase <job.json> [--repo <path>] [--json]` — offline classifier over
  a jobs-API payload, printing class, step, handoff label, owner phrase and an
  explanation, with a permanent "NOT a gate verdict" line.

== 13. One-paragraph summary for the owner

`squabble` is already the estate's most precise instrument for *why a required
context does not exist* (moved coordinates, allow-list admission, dead pins,
non-emitted names) and for *what must never be called green* (non-empty
requirement set, skipped ≠ passed, vacuity). It had no concept of *where a job
stopped*, so a runner or lock failure was attributed to the workflow's shape —
the one misattribution that dispatches real work for a non-code failure. That
gap now has a pure, fail-closed classifier and an offline command, with signatures
in a directive rather than in the engine, and with handoffs that name an owner
class. It is not yet wired into `fight` (P-1, P-2), it does not name the
principal behind an inaccessible setting (P-3), and it still cannot see billing
or lockfile classes that hypatia already owns (P-4). None of this makes anything
green, and none of it should be read as a green.
